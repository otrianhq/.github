# Otrian

**Software products and SaaS platforms for communication, community, and workflow.**

Otrian is a software company founded in **2025 in Germany**. We build and operate focused SaaS products that solve practical communication, community, and operational workflow needs.

## Our products

### [Loghon](https://www.loghon.com/)
Live translation for church services, sermons, liturgy, talks, and meetings. Attendees can follow translated captions and optional audio directly in their browser.

### [UnitedLang](https://www.unitedlang.com/)
Live translation for events, meetings, lectures, and multilingual communication. Hosts share a QR code or browser link so attendees can follow in their preferred language.

### [Churvice](https://www.churvice.com/)
Church data and operations software for managing members and families, ministries, groups, events, attendance, follow-up, permissions, and reporting.

## Engineering at Otrian

We combine product engineering, cloud infrastructure, automation, and applied AI to turn real-world workflows into focused software products.

Our commercial product source repositories are private.
